# Reader Shift — Template

1. Diagnosis: core meaning, reader start state, missing elements, and title assessment.
2. Rewriting strategy: how to connect and fill gaps.
3. Rewritten first-person article.
4. Quality checklist: honesty of title, emotional transition, evidence, and actionable ending.

Guidelines
- Keep the original intent and voice where possible.
- Avoid over-promising outcomes; ensure the reader's next step is realistic and measurable.
